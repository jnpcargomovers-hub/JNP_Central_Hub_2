How to update · कैसे करें

  1. Download the dispatch report (Transporter Portal, from the start of the month / year till today) → in JNP_Finance_DB › Portal_Loading click A1 and paste the whole file over the old one.
  2. Download the Daily DI Follow-up → in Daily Paste click A1 and paste the whole file. The pending LR list on the dashboard updates by itself.
  3. Every 30 minutes the app picks up the new paste by itself: actual MT, PGI, EPOD, bill and freight are filled and trucks move on the board.
  4. Corrections (LR changed, truck or destination different) wait for you: check the list below → tap Apply.
🔒 Each Loader entry is corrected only once. Pasting the same month again, or 3 months at once, never changes an entry that was already matched — later pastes only add EPOD, bill no. and freight.
