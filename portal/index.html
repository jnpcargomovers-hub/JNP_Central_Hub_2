How to update · कैसे करें

  1. Download the dispatch report (Transporter Portal, from the start of the month / year till today) → in JNP_Finance_DB › Portal_Loading click A1 and paste the whole file over the old one.
  2. Download the Daily DI Follow-up → in Daily Paste click A1 and paste the whole file. The pending LR list on the dashboard updates by itself.
  3. Come back here → check → tap Apply to correct the Loader entries.
