Upload portal files · पोर्टल फाइल अपलोड करें

  1. On the UTCL portal download the Dispatch report (any dates — today, the week or 3 months) and the Pending LRs report.
  2. Tap the button and pick the file(s) — you can pick both together. The app knows which is which.
  3. That's all: history is kept, the Loader entries, truck board and dashboard update at once.
📅 Upload the dispatch report every day. The portal searches only 3 months back — every DI you upload stays here for good (JNP_Finance_DB › Portal_Loading). Uploading the same days again is safe: nothing doubles.
🔒 Each Loader entry is corrected only once. Corrections (LR, truck or destination different) still wait for you below → Apply.
